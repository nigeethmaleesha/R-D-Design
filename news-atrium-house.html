The Atrium House

A Contemporary Urban Sanctuary.

News & Views/Studio News/The Atrium House

Overview

A Contemporary Urban Sanctuary.

The Atrium House is a modern reinterpretation of tropical architecture designed as a calm, inward-focused retreat in the heart of Colombo’s urban bustle. This two-story residence is built around a central open air atrium, flooding the home with natural light and ventilation while maintaining privacy from the outside world.

DisciplineArchitecture
SectorResidential
Photography6 images

Design Intent

Calm, Airy, and Rooted in Nature.

The client wanted a home that felt peaceful, airy, and connected to nature without sacrificing contemporary comforts. Our approach centered on integrating an internal courtyard that would become the heart of the home. All major living spaces including the kitchen, lounge, and upstairs bedrooms revolve around this atrium, creating a natural flow and constant visual connection to greenery and sky.

Internal Courtyard Natural Flow Visual Connection

Materials & Elements

Built with Warmth and Spatial Clarity.

  • Locally sourced timber for warmth and texture
  • Polished concrete flooring for durability and thermal mass
  • Vertical timber slats for passive shading
  • Frameless glass panels for unobstructed views
  • Open-plan layout with minimal walls to enhance spatial continuity

Sustainability Features

Quietly Sustainable by Design.

  • Passive cooling through cross-ventilation
  • Rainwater harvesting system integrated into the roof
  • Solar panels positioned for maximum efficiency
  • Use of recycled wood for selected interiors

Outcomes

A Home That Feels Mindful and Meaningful.

The Atrium House stands as a quiet architectural statement minimalist, mindful, and meaningful. It reflects a design philosophy rooted in spatial clarity, sustainability, and emotional comfort.
